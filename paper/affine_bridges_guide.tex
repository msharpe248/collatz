{\Large Guide: Using an Auxiliary Convergent Orbit}\par\medskip
Collatz proof project\quad---\quad September 5, 2026
\end{center}
\guideheading{The proof strategy}
For a natural parameter $u$, the transfer question asks whether convergence
of $3u+2$ implies convergence of $27u+20$. Proving this for every parameter
would prove Collatz. Strong induction lets us use transfer at any strictly
smaller parameter, provided every case is eventually covered.

\guideheading{What the new bridge does}
Start with the known-convergent smaller partner. If its orbit reaches
$3v+2$ for some $v<u$, the induction hypothesis supplies convergence of
$27v+20$. This auxiliary orbit can then meet the original larger partner.
The original two partners do not have to return together to a smaller pair.

Lean verifies this rule and the following infinite family:
\[
 u=28+4096Q,\qquad v=21+3072Q<u.
\]
The smaller partner reaches $3v+2$ after two steps. The original larger
partner after twelve steps and the auxiliary larger partner after ten
steps both equal $47+6561Q$. These identities hold for every natural $Q$.
Transfer at $v$ therefore implies transfer at $u$.

\guideheading{What the experiment adds}
Among parameters 0 through 1000, 812 pairs enter the trivial cycle without
ever meeting the old merge or simultaneous-return criteria. Following
those same pairs for longer cannot produce either old certificate.
This does not make their transfer statements false: both partners converge.
It also does not rule out using finite base cases in an induction.

The expanded search allows up to two auxiliary bridges and thirty ordinary
steps. It finds 599 conditional certificates, including 421 of those 812
previously excluded parameters. It leaves 402 parameters at its depth
limit. These are finite search results, not a proof that every parameter
has a bridge certificate. Larger search limits may change the counts.

\guideheading{What has been checked}
Lean checks the general inference rule, the strict decrease in the displayed
family, and all its orbit identities for arbitrary $Q$. The search records
every recursive bridge and checks its parameter decreases uniformly over
the entire cylinder. Tests replay all saved certificates at four quotients
and compare a small complete search with independent path enumeration.
The whole census has not been certified in Lean.

\guideheading{What remains open}
The smaller transfer premises are still required. We need a proof that
all parameters admit a checked base case or a finite certificate whose
recursive parameters are smaller. The current search does not establish
this coverage. Collatz remains unproved.
